Lovingly designed

A smooth waveform timeline makes searching through your books effortless. Chapters, playback speed, sleep timer and full support for lock screen and Bluetooth controls.

Your library, your files

Import audiobooks straight from your device. Ivy keeps its own copies, remembers where you left off in every book, and picks up right where you stopped.

Clip the moments worth keeping

Save passages as audio clips. Add notes, get automatic on-device transcriptions, and share clips with anyone — no account needed, nothing leaves your phone unless you say so.

Keep your history

Ivy privately keeps track of your listening sessions. See how much you listen by day, week, month, or year, and browse back through every book you've spent time with.

Ivy is private: no accounts, no tracking, no servers.
